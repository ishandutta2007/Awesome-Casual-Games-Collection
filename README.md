# Awesome-Casual-Games-Collection



**Curated List of Commercial Collections & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Classic Puzzle, Card, Board Games & Bite-Sized Arcade Fun*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial casual game collections** and **open-source projects** that bundle classic puzzle, card, board, and arcade games into accessible packages for quick, relaxing play sessions.



**Examples** include Microsoft Casual Games, Clubhouse Games: 51 Worldwide Classics, PopCap Party Pack, Hoyle Card Games, Big Fish Games Collection, Pogo Games, GameHouse Casual Club, PlayFirst Collection, WildTangent Games, and MSN Games Collection (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: The open-source casual games ecosystem is **diverse and production-proven**, with collections spanning web, desktop, and mobile. **AlexGames** bundles 15+ classic games (Solitaire, Chess, Go, Minesweeper, Backgammon, and more) in a single Lua/Rust web-accessible package . **Chogan** delivers tower defense, Sudoku, Minesweeper, and Dots and Boxes in a fully offline, tracker-free Android app with reproducible F-Droid builds . **Fair Games** offers six classic games (Block Blast, Tetris, Flappy Bird, Breakout, Sudoku, 2048) as a free, open-source iOS app with no ads or in-app purchases . **Ace of Penguins** brings 12 solitaire and puzzle games to Debian with a penguin theme . This section documents these production-grade solutions.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Commercial Collections



- **[Microsoft Casual Games](https://zone.msn.com/en-us/microsoft-casual-games)**  

  Microsoft's collection of classic casual games including Solitaire, Minesweeper, Mahjong, and Jigsaw. Free with Windows, with premium ad-free options. **Best for**: Windows users wanting familiar classics.



- **[Clubhouse Games: 51 Worldwide Classics](https://www.nintendo.com/store/products/clubhouse-games-51-worldwide-classics-switch/)**  

  **Nintendo's comprehensive collection of 51 board, card, and table games from around the world.** Includes Chess, Shogi, Riichi Mahjong, Texas Hold'em, Hanafuda, and many more. **MobyGames score: 7.8/10** . **Best for**: Switch owners wanting a diverse collection of classic games with local and online multiplayer.



- **[PopCap Party Pack](https://www.ea.com/games/popcap)**  

  Collection of PopCap's classic casual games including Bejeweled, Peggle, Zuma, and Plants vs. Zombies. **Best for**: Fans of PopCap's polished puzzle and arcade titles.



- **[Hoyle Card Games](https://www.hoylegames.com/)**  

  Long-running series of card game collections featuring Poker, Bridge, Euchre, Hearts, and more with tutorials. **Best for**: Card game enthusiasts wanting variety with teaching tools.



- **[Big Fish Games Collection](https://www.bigfishgames.com/)**  

  Subscription-based collection of casual games including hidden object, puzzle, and time management titles. **Best for**: Players wanting a large rotating library of casual games.



- **[Pogo Games](https://www.pogo.com/)**  

  EA's online casual games portal featuring Solitaire, Mahjong, Word Whomp, and more. Free with ads, or subscription for ad-free play. **Best for**: Online casual gaming with a social element.



- **[GameHouse Casual Club](https://www.gamehouse.com/)**  

  Subscription service with access to a library of casual games including time management, puzzle, and hidden object titles. **Best for**: Players wanting a diverse rotating collection.



- **[PlayFirst Collection](https://www.playfirst.com/)**  

  Collection featuring Diner Dash and other casual time management games. **Best for**: Fans of time management and simulation games.



- **[WildTangent Games](https://www.wildtangent.com/)**  

  Casual game portal with a large library of downloadable and browser-based games. **Best for**: Windows users wanting a broad selection of casual titles.



- **[MSN Games Collection](https://zone.msn.com/en-us/games)**  

  Microsoft's web-based casual games portal featuring Solitaire, Mahjong, and more. Free with ads. **Best for**: Browser-based quick play without installation.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



### Multi-Game Collections



- **[AlexGames](https://github.com/alexbarry/AlexGames)**  

  **A collection of simple Lua and Rust games playable on web and more.** **Key features**: **15+ classic games** including Solitaire (Klondike), Chess, Go/Weiqi/Baduk, Reversi, Checkers/Draughts, Backgammon, Cribbage, Gomoku, Minesweeper, FreeCell, Gem Match, Word Mastermind, Crossword Letters, Endless Runner, Spider Swing, Thrust, and Fluid Mix . **English dictionary** for word puzzles; **WebSocket multiplayer**; **state sharing via URL**; **auto-saving with undo/redo**; **upload your own Lua games** . **Play**: Web version available at alexbarry.github.io/AlexGames . **Best for**: Users wanting a diverse collection of classic games in a single web-accessible package.



- **[Chogan](https://github.com/choganhq/chogan)**  

  **Offline game collection: tower defence, sudoku, minesweeper, dots and boxes.** **Key features**: **Tower Defence** (four towers, three fixed maps plus a daily map, twenty waves, endless mode); **Sudoku** (four difficulties with guaranteed unique solution, notes, hints, daily challenge); **Minesweeper** (no-guess generation, four sizes, chording and flags, daily challenge); **Dots and Boxes** (two players on one phone, or three levels of AI) . **Daily challenge** identical on every device with no server; **weekly offline league** with five tiers; **30+ awards**; **shared coin** for hints and cosmetics; **Persian, English, and Simplified Chinese** interface with light and dark themes . **No permissions** — Android manifest declares only VIBRATE, not even internet access; all assets ship inside the package; progress stored on device only . **F-Droid reproducible build** — both sources ship identical signed APK . **MIT licensed** . **Best for**: Privacy-conscious Android and web users wanting a polished, tracker-free game collection.



- **[Fair Games](https://apps.apple.com/dk/app/fair-games/id6761661340)**  

  **Free, open-source collection of classic puzzle and arcade games for iOS.** **Key features**: **Block Blast** (drop and arrange block shapes on 8x8 grid); **Sirtet** (falling-block game); **Flappy Bird** (tap to flap through pipes); **Breakout** (bounce ball to smash bricks); **Sudoku** (four difficulty levels, hints, pencil marks, undo/redo, timer); **2048** (swipe to merge tiles) . **Features**: Satisfying haptic feedback; high score tracking; game state persistence; pause menu; dark theme; auto-save; **no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking** . **100% open source**; distributed through App Fair and built with Skip . **Best for**: iOS users wanting a free, ad-free, open-source collection of classic games.



### Solitaire & Card Game Collections



- **[Ace of Penguins](https://packages.debian.org/ja/buster/ace-of-penguins)**  

  **Penguin-themed solitaire games for Debian and Linux.** **Key features**: **12 games** including Canfield, Freecell, Golf, Mastermind, Merlin, Minesweeper, Pegged, Solitaire, Spider, Taipei (with level editor), and Thornq . **Enhancements** over classic Windows versions . **Best for**: Linux users wanting a comprehensive solitaire collection with penguin charm.



- **[Solitaire Pack -- Lite](https://apps.apple.com/bs/app/solitaire-pack-lite/id648930700)**  

  **Combines six classic Solitaire games in a tiny download package.** **Games**: Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, Tri-Peaks, and two flavors of Pyramid . **Improvements for mobile**: Drag entire column instead of individual cards; simplified scoring (moves only); hints and undo in all games . **Best for**: Mobile users wanting a compact, feature-rich solitaire collection.



### Game Remakes & Ports



- **[PvZ-Portable](https://github.com/wszqkzqk/PvZ-Portable)**  

  **A cross-platform reimplementation of Plants vs. Zombies by the community.** **Key features**: **100% authentic experience of GOTY edition**; **portable save format** (`.v4`) using Type-Length-Value serialization — saves transfer between x86_64, ARM, RISC-V, and LoongArch . **Save editing tool** (`pvzp-v4-converter.py`) for inspecting and modifying `.v4` files . **Build options** include cheat keys, bug fixes, console, and static linking . **Best for**: PvZ fans wanting a portable, community-maintained version of the classic game.



- **[JMines](https://github.com/stephanebruckert/JMines)**  

  **Remake of 1on1 Minesweeper Flags by MSN Games.** **18 stars**; Java-based . **Best for**: Fans of the classic MSN Minesweeper multiplayer variant.



- **[cards2.0](https://github.com/T1mmos/cards2.0)**  

  **Remake of MSN Messenger's Solitaire Showdown.** **9 stars**; updated 11 months ago . **Best for**: Nostalgic users wanting to relive MSN Messenger's Solitaire Showdown.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Casual games handle potentially sensitive user data; review privacy policies and permission requests before installing, especially on mobile devices.

- **Open-source reality**: The open-source ecosystem for casual games collections is **diverse and production-proven**. **AlexGames** bundles 15+ classic games with multiplayer and custom Lua game support . **Chogan** delivers a fully offline, tracker-free collection with reproducible F-Droid builds and no internet permission . **Fair Games** provides six classic games for iOS with no ads or tracking . **Ace of Penguins** brings 12 solitaire games to Debian and Linux . **PvZ-Portable** reimplements Plants vs. Zombies with portable save formats across architectures . However, **commercial collections** (Clubhouse Games: 51 Worldwide Classics, Microsoft Casual Games) provide **curated experiences with polished presentation and cross-platform multiplayer** that open-source alternatives may not fully match. The open-source path is **genuinely viable** for users wanting free, privacy-respecting collections of classic games across web, desktop, and mobile.
